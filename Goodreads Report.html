The Cider House Rules A decade-spanning Bildungsroman told with Dickensian verve. The story is set principally in the orphanage of St Cloud's in Maine before WWII and follows the lives of its staff and its wards until some time in the 1950s. Principal among these are orphan Homer Wells and ether-addicted obstetrician Dr Wilbur Larch; thw two form a proxy father-son relationship as Larch seeks to pass down his medical knowledge and one day find his replacement. A key theme of the book is abortion - a service offered unofficially by St Cloud's - and Homer's ethical grappling with the subject. The book is long but worth it for the rich characterisation./>/> 5
